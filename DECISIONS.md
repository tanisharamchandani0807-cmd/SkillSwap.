# SkillSwap — Decision Points

## Hackathon ID

`AZIS-XB92FA`

## DP1 — Rejection

**Chosen behavior:** Keep the declined booking visible in My Bookings with a **Declined** status and provide a **Find Similar Gigs** action.

**Why:** This gives the client transparency about what happened and an immediate alternative path. Removing the booking would make the history unclear and leave the client without a next step.

## DP2 — Double Booking

**Chosen behavior:** Allow multiple booking requests to remain **Pending** for the same gig. The creator reviews each request and can Accept or Decline it.

**Why:** A pending request is not yet a confirmed booking, so it should not automatically block other opportunities. This also gives creators flexibility to choose the request that best fits their availability and project requirements.

## DP3 — Discovery

**Chosen behavior:** Rank gigs **newest first** by default, with search and category filtering.

**Why:** Newest-first discovery gives newly listed creators a fair chance to appear near the top. Search and category filters then help clients quickly find the services most relevant to them.
